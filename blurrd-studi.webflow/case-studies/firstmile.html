Our Involvement:

FirstMile.com was slow, clunky, and difficult to use, even for the internal team. To solve this, we migrated to Webflow, taking advantage of its bulk upload feature for a swift transfer from WordPress. Inspired by GitHub and Shopify's designs, we created a sleek, user-friendly layout. Through close collaboration with leadership, we built a site that dramatically improved performance, SEO, and user experience. The switch to Webflow transformed the site into a fast, efficient platform, delivering incredible results.
