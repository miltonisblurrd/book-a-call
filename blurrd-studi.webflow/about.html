What is BLURRD Studio?

Founded in 2016, BLURRD Studio is a creative studio partnering with companies, artists, and startups to craft branding, design, and development into thoughtful, high-performing digital experiences.

My Experience + Workflow

I keep things intentionally small so I can go deep with every project—high attention, tight collaboration, and work that doesn’t just look good, it performs. Whether we’re building your brand from scratch or launching your next product, the process stays thoughtful, strategic, and built around momentum. No fluff, no bloat.

Meetings stay minimal. Most progress happens async through Slack, email, Loom, and Notion so you can review on your time. I only schedule calls when they’re truly needed—direction, major reviews, launch readiness. You stay informed and involved without losing your week.
