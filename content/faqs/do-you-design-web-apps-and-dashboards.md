Yes. Web and product design covers the interfaces your users and teams actually live in — not just landing pages.

## What I design

**Customer-facing product**
- Web apps and SaaS dashboards
- Onboarding, settings, billing, and core workflow screens
- Marketing sites and product landing pages that match the in-app experience

**Internal and operational tools**
- Admin panels and reporting views
- Data-heavy layouts with clear hierarchy
- Role-based UI patterns when different users see different states

## How complex products stay usable

Dashboards fail when everything competes for attention. Good product design prioritizes the job each screen needs to do, uses consistent components, and plans for density without clutter.
