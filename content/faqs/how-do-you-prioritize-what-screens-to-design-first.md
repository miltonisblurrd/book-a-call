I prioritize by impact: the screens tied to activation, conversion, and daily usage. I map the core journey and identify where users make decisions or get stuck. Then I design those screens first so testing and development can start sooner. Secondary screens come later once the core flow is solid. This keeps projects lean and focused on outcomes.
