Yes. Development spans marketing presence and product execution — whatever you need to put in front of users with confidence. That includes high-converting marketing sites, authenticated web apps, SaaS dashboards, and MVPs scoped for real user testing instead of endless prototyping.

## What I build

**Marketing and product sites**
- Landing pages and full marketing sites built for conversion and SEO
- Webflow, Next.js, and custom front-end builds matched to your team
- Sites that reflect your brand positioning and connect to product flows

**Web apps and dashboards**
- SaaS front-ends with auth, settings, billing, and core workflows
- Dashboards and internal tools with data-heavy, usable layouts
- MVPs designed to ship, learn, and iterate — not sit in staging

## How I choose the stack

The right stack depends on your team, timeline, and growth plans — not a one-size-fits-all template. Common paths include Next.js for product-grade apps, Webflow for marketing velocity, and headless setups when you need both performance and content flexibility. Every build prioritizes clean handoff, maintainable code, and fast loads.
