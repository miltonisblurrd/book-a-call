Yes. I can redesign and migrate your site while preserving SEO basics and improving structure. Whether you are moving to Webflow, a headless CMS, or a custom Next.js build, I handle the transition so you do not lose momentum after launch.

## What migration includes

**Before the move**
- Audit of existing pages, content, and URL structure
- Redirect mapping to protect search rankings
- Content cleanup — removing outdated pages and consolidating where it helps

**During the rebuild**
- Redesign opportunity: improve layout, typography, and performance
- CMS setup so your team can update content without touching code
- QA across devices, forms, and key conversion flows

**After launch**
- Redirect verification and basic SEO checks
- Handoff documentation for your team
- Post-launch fixes for critical issues in the launch window

## Why this matters

Bad migrations lose traffic, break links, and leave you with a site that is harder to manage than before. A planned migration protects what you have while setting you up to grow.
