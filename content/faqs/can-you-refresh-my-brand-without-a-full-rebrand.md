Yes — and for many companies, a refresh is the smarter move. If your core message and positioning are still right but the visuals feel dated or inconsistent, I can modernize the identity without throwing away the brand equity you have already built.

## Refresh vs full rebrand

**Brand refresh (what stays)**
- Core positioning, name, and audience definition
- Existing brand recognition and customer trust
- Logo structure, updated with refined typography and color

**Brand refresh (what changes)**
- Visual system: type scale, color palette, layout rules
- Photography, illustration, and iconography direction
- Updated guidelines and asset kit for consistent rollout

**Full rebrand (when you need it)**
- Positioning no longer matches the product or market
- Name, category, or audience has fundamentally shifted
- Legacy identity is actively hurting credibility or conversion

## How I approach a refresh

I audit what is working, identify what feels off, and update the system so every touchpoint feels current — web, product UI, decks, and social included.
