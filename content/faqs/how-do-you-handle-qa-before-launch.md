QA is where small details become professional results. Before anything goes live, I run through a structured checklist so you launch with confidence — not a list of bugs your users find first.

## What I test before launch

**Functionality**
- Navigation, links, forms, and interactive elements across the site or app
- Auth flows, account settings, and role-based UI states
- CMS content, dynamic pages, and API-driven data rendering

**Cross-device and browser**
- Responsive behavior on mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Testing on major browsers: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge
- Touch interactions, scroll behavior, and viewport edge cases

**Performance and SEO basics**
- Page load speed and Core Web Vitals pass
- Metadata, Open Graph tags, and structured data where relevant
- Redirects, sitemap, and robots.txt for migrations or rebuilds

## How issues get resolved

I fix what I find before launch — not after. For anything requiring backend changes, I document clearly so your team can address it quickly. You get a launch-ready product, not a beta disguised as a release.
