The handoff is where many projects break down. I design with development in mind from the start — not as an afterthought export at the end.

## What dev teams receive

**Organized Figma structure**
- Named pages and frames by flow or feature
- Shared components, styles, and spacing tokens
- Responsive layouts for the breakpoints you ship

**Real states included**
- Empty, loading, error, and success states
- Form validation and edge cases where they affect UX
- Notes on interaction and behavior when it is not obvious from static screens

**Optional continuity into build**
- If you want one partner from design through development, I can carry the work into [web and product development](/services/development) — same taste, fewer translation gaps

## Why this reduces rework

When engineers reverse-engineer design intent from messy files, you pay twice: in build time and in UI polish that never quite matches the mockup. Clean handoff keeps velocity high after design sign-off.
