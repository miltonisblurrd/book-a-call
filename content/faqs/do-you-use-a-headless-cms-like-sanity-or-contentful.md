Yes, when you need flexible content and a custom front end. I set up schemas, content models, and a clean editing experience so your team can update pages, blog posts, and resources without touching code.

## When a headless CMS makes sense

**Good fit**
- Marketing sites that need frequent content updates
- Blogs, resources, case studies, and landing pages managed by non-developers
- Custom front ends where a traditional CMS theme would limit design
- Multi-channel content: web, app, and email from one source

**What I set up**
- Content models and schemas tailored to your page types
- Sanity Studio or Contentful configured for your team's workflow
- Front-end integration with Next.js, Astro, or your chosen framework
- Preview and publishing workflows so content goes live cleanly

## Why this matters

A headless CMS separates content from presentation. Your team gets an easy editing experience, and your site gets a custom front end that performs well and looks exactly how you designed it.
