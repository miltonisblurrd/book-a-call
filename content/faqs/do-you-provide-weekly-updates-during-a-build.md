Yes. I send consistent weekly updates so you always know where the build stands, what shipped, what is coming next, and what I need from you to keep momentum.

## What each update includes

**Progress and context**
- What was completed since the last update
- What is in progress and what is blocked
- Upcoming milestones and review dates

**Async review when it helps**
- Loom walkthroughs for design decisions or complex flows
- Links to staging previews so you can test on your own time
- Clear asks when a decision or asset is needed from your team

## Why weekly updates matter

Surprises near launch are expensive. Regular updates keep stakeholders aligned, reduce back-and-forth, and make it easier to catch scope drift early. You stay informed without sitting in status meetings all week. Most clients review updates in under ten minutes and reply only when a decision is needed.
