Development is an investment in something you can ship — not a line item that disappears into technical debt. Pricing depends on what you are building, how complex the integrations are, and how launch-ready your design and content are on day one. I scope in sprints so scope and investment stay aligned as the project evolves.

## How pricing works

Projects are scoped in **sprints** based on:
- New build vs rebuild or migration from an existing product
- Number of pages, screens, or product flows in scope
- Integrations: CMS, auth, payments, APIs, analytics, email
- Design readiness — build-ready Figma vs design still in progress

## What drives the number

The scope conversation covers:
- Marketing site vs full web app with authenticated users
- Platform choice, hosting, and long-term maintenance expectations
- Content, copy, and asset readiness at kickoff
- Timeline, launch constraints, and phased delivery options

## Getting a right-sized quote

You do not need a full technical spec to start. A short discovery call is enough to recommend the right build plan — a launch-ready MVP, a marketing site, or a phased rebuild that spreads investment across milestones.
