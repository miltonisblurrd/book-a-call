Development is an investment in something you can ship — not a line item that disappears into technical debt.

## How pricing works

Projects are scoped in **sprints** based on:
- New build vs rebuild or migration
- Number of pages, screens, or product flows
- Integrations (CMS, auth, payments, APIs, analytics)
- Design readiness — build-ready Figma vs design-in-progress

## What drives the number

The scope conversation covers:
- Marketing site vs full web app
- Platform choice and hosting requirements
- Content and asset readiness
- Timeline and launch constraints

## Getting a right-sized quote

You do not need a full technical spec to start. A short discovery call is enough to recommend the right build plan: a launch-ready MVP, a marketing site, or a phased rebuild.
