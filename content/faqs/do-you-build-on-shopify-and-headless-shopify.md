Yes. I can build Shopify storefronts, custom themes, or headless setups when performance and design flexibility matter. I choose the approach based on your product, team, and how much control you need over the front end.

## Shopify options

**Standard Shopify themes**
- Custom theme design and development on Shopify's platform
- Product pages, collections, cart, and checkout styled to match your brand
- Good fit when you want Shopify's native admin and app ecosystem

**Headless Shopify**
- Hydrogen or custom front end connected to Shopify's Storefront API
- Full design control without theme limitations
- Better performance through static generation and edge delivery
- Good fit when your brand experience needs to go beyond what a theme allows

## What I handle

- Store setup, product structure, and collection organization
- Custom checkout styling and cart experience where scoped
- Integration with your marketing site, email, and analytics tools
- Performance and mobile QA before launch

## Why this matters

Shopify's default themes work for basic stores. When your brand and product experience need to feel premium — and perform well on mobile — a custom or headless build is worth the investment.
