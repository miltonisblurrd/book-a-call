I avoid chasing surface-level trends and focus on timeless principles like hierarchy, typography, and proportion. I use trends only when they reinforce the story and fit your category. I also test the system across multiple contexts so it is not dependent on one layout or one graphic style. Then I document rules so future updates stay consistent. The result feels modern now and stable later.
