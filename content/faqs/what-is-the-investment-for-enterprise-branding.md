Branding is an investment in leverage: clearer positioning, stronger first impressions, and a system that reduces rework across web, product, and sales.

## Typical starting point

Brand identity projects typically start at **$7,500+** depending on scope.

Investment increases when you need:
- A full identity rebuild vs a focused refresh
- Multiple logo explorations and stakeholder review rounds
- Extended asset kits (product UI, campaign templates, sales materials)
- Tighter timelines with parallel workstreams

## What drives the number

I price based on outcomes and complexity, not vague hourly estimates. The scope conversation covers:
- New brand vs rebrand
- Number of deliverables and templates required
- How many teams will apply the system (marketing, product, sales)
- Timeline and feedback structure

## Getting a right-sized quote

You do not need a perfect brief to start. A short discovery call is enough to recommend the right level of work: full package for a new launch, or a scoped refresh if you already have equity worth keeping.
