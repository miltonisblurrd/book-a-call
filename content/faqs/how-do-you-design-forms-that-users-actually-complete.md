I simplify forms by reducing fields, grouping inputs logically, and using clear labels. I also design helpful validation states so users fix issues quickly. For long forms, I use steps and progress cues to reduce drop-off. I test on mobile to ensure spacing and keyboard behavior are comfortable. Better forms usually mean more signups and fewer abandoned actions.
