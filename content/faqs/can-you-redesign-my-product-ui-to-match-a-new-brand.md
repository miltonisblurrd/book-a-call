Yes. I translate your new identity into real UI decisions: typography scale, spacing rhythm, color usage, and component styling. A rebrand that stops at the marketing site leaves users feeling a jarring mismatch the moment they log in. The product is where trust is tested daily — it needs to feel like the same company as the homepage.

## How I approach product UI after a rebrand

**Translate identity into UI tokens**
- Type scales, color roles, and border radius mapped to components
- Icon and illustration style applied to in-app surfaces
- Brand personality expressed through density, contrast, and motion — not just logos

**Prioritize high-impact flows first**
- Onboarding, dashboard home, settings, and billing updated early
- Screens users see most often aligned before edge-case polish
- Quick wins that make the product feel cohesive while deeper work continues

**Roll out without breaking velocity**
- Phased plan your engineering team can ship incrementally
- Component updates that reduce one-off screen rebuilds
- Design files organized so devs know what changes now vs later

The goal is one cohesive experience. Users should never feel a mismatch between your site and your product.
