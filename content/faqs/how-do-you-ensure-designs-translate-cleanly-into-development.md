I design with development constraints in mind and keep components reusable. I annotate interactions, states, and responsive behavior so implementation is clear. I also avoid one-off magic layouts that are hard to replicate across screens. When needed, I provide a handoff checklist and a short walkthrough. Clean translation reduces bugs and saves time.
