Yes — and I recommend it often. Starting with a focused sprint or audit is the fastest way to clarify direction before committing to a full branding, design, or development engagement. You get real output, real feedback, and a clearer picture of what the bigger project actually needs.

## Good entry points

**Brand audit or positioning sprint**
- Review your current identity, messaging, and competitive gap
- Deliver a prioritized list of what to fix first
- Decide whether you need a refresh or a full rebrand

**Design sprint on one flow**
- Redesign onboarding, checkout, or a key dashboard view
- Test the new direction before scaling across the product
- Build confidence with stakeholders and users

**Technical discovery or prototype**
- Scope integrations, API requirements, and stack decisions
- Ship a working prototype or landing page in one sprint
- De-risk the full build with working code, not slides

## Why this works

Small starts reduce risk, create momentum, and give both of us a chance to confirm the fit before a larger commitment. You are never locked into a path you have not tested.
