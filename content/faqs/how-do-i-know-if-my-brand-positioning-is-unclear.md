If people ask "so what do you actually do?" after seeing your homepage or hearing your pitch, your positioning needs work. Unclear positioning is one of the most expensive problems a startup can have — because every marketing dollar and sales conversation starts at a disadvantage.

## Common warning signs

**Customer confusion**
- Prospects compare you to the wrong competitors
- Your homepage tries to explain everything instead of one clear promise
- Demo requests come from the wrong audience or drop off quickly

**Internal inconsistency**
- Sales, marketing, and product describe the company differently
- Every new deck or landing page starts from scratch
- You keep rewriting the tagline because nothing feels right

**Weak conversion signals**
- High traffic but low signup or demo conversion
- Long sales cycles because buyers need extra education
- Strong product reviews but weak top-of-funnel traction

I tighten positioning by defining your audience, promise, proof, and differentiation — then make sure every touchpoint tells the same story. Clarity is not a copywriting trick; it is a strategic decision about who you are for and why you win.
