Yes — when naming is part of the brand problem, not a separate creative exercise. I help you find names that are clear, ownable, and aligned with your positioning — so the name works in a pitch, on a homepage, and in everyday conversation.

## How naming fits into branding

**Strategy-first approach**
- The name should express your positioning, not just sound cool
- Shortlist generation based on audience, category, and tone
- Testing names in real contexts: homepage headline, email subject, verbal pitch

**Practical checks**
- Domain availability and obvious spelling confusion
- Search results and competitor overlap review
- Pronunciation and memorability with real people, not just the team

**Deliverables**
- Curated shortlist with rationale for each option
- Recommended primary name with tagline direction
- Guidance on trademark next steps (I am not a lawyer, but I flag obvious risks)

## When naming is not the priority

If your current name has equity and the problem is messaging or visuals, a refresh may be more valuable than a rename. I will tell you honestly which path makes sense.
