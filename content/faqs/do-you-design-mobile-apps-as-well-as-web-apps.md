Yes. I design iOS and mobile-first experiences alongside web apps and dashboards. I focus on navigation, spacing, and touch-friendly patterns that feel native — not like a shrunken desktop site.

## What mobile design includes

**Platform-aware UI**
- iOS conventions for navigation, tab bars, modals, and gestures
- Mobile-first layouts with thumb-friendly tap targets and spacing
- Responsive web experiences that work well on phones and tablets

**Real-device thinking**
- Flows designed for one-handed use and small screens
- States for loading, errors, permissions, and offline scenarios
- Handoff specs that account for safe areas, scroll behavior, and transitions
- Dark mode and accessibility considerations built into the system from the start

## Why this matters

Mobile is where most users first experience your product. When the UI ignores platform patterns or feels cramped, people bounce before they understand the value. Good mobile design removes friction, builds trust fast, and sets the tone for every session that follows.
