I keep communication simple and consistent so you never have to guess where things stand. You get async updates between meetings, plus scheduled check-ins when decisions need to happen together. No radio silence, no surprise deliverables.

## How I keep you in the loop

**Channels that fit your team**
- Notion for milestones, deliverables, and shared context
- Slack for quick questions and day-to-day coordination
- Loom for walkthroughs when async review is faster than a meeting

**Rhythm that protects momentum**
- Weekly status updates with what shipped, what is next, and any blockers
- Review checkpoints tied to phases — not open-ended feedback loops
- Clear asks when I need assets, approvals, or decisions from your side

## Why this matters

Vague communication creates rework and timeline drift. When you know what is happening and what you need to do, projects move faster and feel less stressful for everyone involved. I have seen too many projects stall because nobody knew who was waiting on whom — that does not happen here.
