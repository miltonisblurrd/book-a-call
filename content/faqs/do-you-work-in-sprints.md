Yes. Sprints help me ship faster by keeping scope focused and feedback tight. They work well for MVPs, landing pages, and redesigns that need momentum — not open-ended projects with no end date.

## How sprints work

**Structure**
- Fixed timeline — typically one to three weeks per sprint
- Defined deliverables agreed before the sprint starts
- Daily or every-other-day async updates so nothing stalls

**What sprints are good for**
- MVP landing pages and test-ready builds
- Redesigns of a focused section or core flow
- Brand and design work with a hard launch deadline

**What sprints are not**
- Full product builds with undefined scope
- Projects waiting on slow stakeholder approval at every step
- Open-ended exploration without a clear output
- A substitute for proper discovery when the problem is not yet defined

## Why this matters

Sprints force clarity. When scope, timeline, and deliverables are locked upfront, you get real output fast — instead of weeks of meetings with nothing shipped.
