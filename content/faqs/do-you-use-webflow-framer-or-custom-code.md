I use all three — and the right choice depends on your goals, team, and how you plan to maintain the site after launch. There is no universal best platform; there is the best platform for your situation.

## When each option fits

**Webflow**
- Marketing sites with CMS-driven pages your team updates regularly
- Strong design control without a dedicated engineering team
- Fast launches with solid hosting and SEO out of the box

**Framer**
- Design-forward marketing sites with rich animations and interactions
- Teams that want a visual editor with modern publishing workflows
- Landing pages and product marketing sites that need to look exceptional

**Custom code (Next.js, headless CMS, etc.)**
- Web apps, dashboards, and products that need custom logic
- Complex integrations, auth, and API-driven experiences
- Teams with engineering resources who want full control and scalability

## How I decide

I recommend the stack during discovery based on maintenance capacity, integration needs, performance requirements, and timeline — not personal preference. The goal is a site or product your team can actually run after I hand it off.
