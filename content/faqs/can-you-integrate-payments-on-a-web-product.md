Yes, depending on your stack. I integrate Stripe checkout flows and build front-end pricing and billing UI so the path to payment is clear, trustworthy, and tested across every state.

## What payment integration covers

**Pricing and checkout UX**
- Pricing pages with clear tier comparison and feature breakdowns
- Checkout flows that reduce friction and build confidence before charge
- Upgrade, downgrade, and plan-change paths that do not confuse users

**States that often get skipped**
- Success confirmations with clear next steps
- Failed payment handling with recovery options
- Cancellation and refund flows that stay respectful and on brand
- Loading and pending states so users never wonder if something broke

## Billing is product UX, not just engineering

A technically working payment flow that feels sketchy will cost you conversions. I treat billing as part of the product experience and keep it clean and reliable. Stripe is the most common integration, but I can work with your existing billing setup when needed.
