If priorities shift, I pause and reassess scope and timeline. I identify what can be swapped, what must be added, and what needs to move to a later phase. Then I update the plan and pricing if required so expectations stay clear. Flexibility is fine when it is handled transparently. This prevents scope creep from quietly killing quality.
