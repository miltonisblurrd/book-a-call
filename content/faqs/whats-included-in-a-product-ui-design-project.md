Product UI design is not a pretty mockup in isolation. I deliver key screens, responsive layouts, components, states, and interaction notes in Figma — organized for handoff so development is straightforward and consistent.

## What you receive

**Discovery + direction**
- Alignment on users, core flows, and what to design first
- Audit of existing UI if you are redesigning
- Flow mapping for the highest-impact paths

**UI design deliverables**
- High-fidelity screens for web apps, dashboards, or product pages
- Component library: buttons, inputs, navigation, cards, modals
- Empty, loading, error, and edge states — not just happy paths
- Responsive layouts planned for real breakpoints

**Handoff**
- Organized Figma files with styles, components, and clear naming
- Interaction and behavior notes where they affect the build
- Prototypes for stakeholder review or user testing when scoped

## Why this matters

When design files are messy or incomplete, engineering fills gaps with inconsistent decisions. Build-ready design reduces rework and gets your product in front of users faster.
