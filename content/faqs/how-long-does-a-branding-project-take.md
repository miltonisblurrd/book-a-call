Most brand identity projects take **4 to 6 weeks** depending on scope, stakeholder count, and how quickly feedback comes back. I move fast by aligning on strategy early and keeping every review cycle structured — so you are not stuck in endless rounds of vague opinions.

## Typical phases

**Week 1–2: Strategy + direction**
- Discovery on audience, positioning, and competitive landscape
- Moodboards and visual direction before any logo work starts
- Alignment on voice, tone, and brand personality

**Week 3–4: Identity design**
- Logo exploration, refinement, and final suite
- Typography, color system, and layout rules
- Early applications on web and product mockups

**Week 5–6: System + handoff**
- Brand guidelines written for real teams
- Asset kit exported in the formats you need
- Final review and rollout support

## What affects the timeline

Larger teams, naming work, or multi-product identity systems can extend the schedule. Smaller refreshes with clear direction often ship in three to four weeks.
