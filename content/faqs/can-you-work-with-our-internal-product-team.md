Yes. Many product orgs already have PMs, designers, or engineers in place — they need senior design direction, speed, or a focused sprint without hiring full-time.

## How collaboration works

**Clear roles from day one**
- I lead UI/UX direction, exploration, and high-fidelity design
- Your team provides product context, user research, and engineering constraints
- Handoff includes Figma files your team can extend after the engagement

**Built for how your team ships**
- Component libraries aligned to your existing system when one exists
- Files organized for design ops, not one-off artboards
- Dev-friendly specs so engineering is not guessing at spacing or behavior

**Communication that respects busy calendars**
- Structured review cycles instead of open-ended workshops
- Async updates with documented decisions
- Fast iteration without pulling every stakeholder into every review

## When this model fits best

- You have a product team but need senior UI direction for a launch or redesign
- You are between design hires and need momentum now
- You want design that translates cleanly into your engineering stack
