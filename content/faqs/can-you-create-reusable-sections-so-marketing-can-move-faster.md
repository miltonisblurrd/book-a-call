Yes. I build modular page sections that marketing can rearrange without breaking design quality. Your team ships new pages faster without waiting on a designer for every campaign or product update.

## What modular sections include

**Flexible blocks built for reuse**
- Feature grids, testimonial rows, pricing tables, and CTA bands
- Consistent spacing, typography, and responsive behavior across every block
- CMS fields structured so editors pick layouts, not rebuild pages from scratch

**Documentation your team can follow**
- Guidelines for when to use each section type
- Image sizing, copy length, and spacing rules to stay on brand
- Examples of good and bad combinations so quality stays high

## Why this matters long term

When every page is a one-off, marketing slows down and brand drift creeps in. Reusable sections reduce dependency on constant design requests while keeping the site feeling intentional. The goal is speed without the sloppy look that usually comes with it. Your marketing team gets autonomy; your brand stays intact.
