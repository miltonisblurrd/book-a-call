Sometimes, yes — if scope is tight and decisions are fast. I run focused sprints to design, build, and ship a test-ready version without bloated features or endless revision cycles.

## What makes a two-week launch possible

**Scope that fits the timeline**
- One core flow or landing page — not a full product with every edge case
- Content and assets ready before the sprint starts
- Pre-existing brand direction or design system to build from

**How I work in sprint mode**
- Daily async updates and quick decision checkpoints
- Design and build in parallel where possible
- Launch with the essentials: responsive layout, core interactions, and basic analytics

## What to expect

You will not get a fully featured product in two weeks. You will get something real enough to test with users, share with investors, or validate a campaign — without cutting corners on quality for what is in scope. Speed and quality are not opposites when scope is honest.
