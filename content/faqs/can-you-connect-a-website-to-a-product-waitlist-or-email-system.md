Yes. I connect your site to a waitlist or email system so traffic turns into a list you can nurture. I integrate with tools like ConvertKit, Mailchimp, or custom APIs depending on your stack.

## What the integration includes

**Form experience that converts**
- On-brand signup flows with minimal fields and clear value props
- Mobile-friendly layouts with comfortable spacing and keyboard behavior
- Success states and error handling that feel polished, not broken

**Backend connection and tracking**
- API or webhook integration with your email platform of choice
- UTM and event tracking so you know which pages and campaigns drive signups
- Spam protection and validation without adding friction for real users

## Why the details matter

Waitlists work best when signup feels effortless. A clunky form or a silent failure after submit kills momentum fast. I design and build the full flow so it feels seamless end to end, from the CTA through confirmation and into your email tool.
