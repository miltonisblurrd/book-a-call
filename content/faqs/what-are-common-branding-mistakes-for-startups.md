Most startup branding fails are not design problems — they are strategy problems wearing a nice font. The biggest mistakes I see are skipping positioning, copying trends, and treating branding as a logo exercise instead of a business decision.

## Mistakes I see constantly

**Skipping strategy**
- Jumping to logo design before defining audience and differentiation
- Building a visual identity that looks good but says nothing specific
- Creating assets that do not translate across web, product, and sales

**Chasing trends**
- Gradient logos and generic SaaS aesthetics that blend into the category
- Copying competitor visual language instead of finding your own lane
- Redesigning every six months because nothing feels "right"

**Inconsistent rollout**
- A polished homepage with off-brand product UI and messy decks
- No guidelines, so every team member interprets the brand differently
- Marketing campaigns that feel disconnected from the product experience

## How to avoid them

Start with positioning: who you serve, what you promise, and why you win. Then design an identity system — not just a logo — that your whole team can deploy consistently.
