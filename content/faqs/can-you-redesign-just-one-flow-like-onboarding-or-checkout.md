Yes. Redesigning a single high-impact flow is often the smartest move when conversion or usability is the main problem — and you do not need a full product overhaul to see results. I map the current path, identify where users drop off, redesign the experience, and validate before handoff.

## How a focused flow redesign works

**Audit the current experience**
- Walk through the flow as a new user would
- Flag friction points: unclear copy, too many steps, missing feedback
- Review analytics or support tickets if you have them

**Redesign with intent**
- Simplify steps without removing necessary information
- Improve hierarchy, microcopy, and error states
- Prototype the new flow for quick stakeholder or user feedback

**Hand off for implementation**
- Annotated files your engineering team can ship
- Component specs that fit your existing design system
- Before-and-after documentation for the team

## Flows that benefit most

Onboarding, checkout, signup, password reset, and empty states are common targets. Fixing one painful flow often lifts metrics across the product.
