Yes. Sprint pricing works when scope is tight and the outcome is clear. You get speed with boundaries instead of an open-ended engagement that grows every week.

## How sprints are structured

**Defined before work starts**
- Exactly what is included and what is out of scope
- A fixed deliverable list with a clear end date
- One or two review rounds built in so feedback stays focused

**Common sprint use cases**
- Landing pages for a launch or campaign
- MVP UI for a core flow or onboarding experience
- Brand or UX audits with a prioritized action list
- Focused rebuilds of a single section or product area

## Why sprints beat vague scopes

Open-ended projects drift. Sprints force clarity upfront, which means faster decisions and a cleaner finish. You know what you are paying for and what you will have in hand when the sprint ends. Most sprints run one to three weeks depending on scope.
