I start with a kickoff that clarifies goals, success metrics, timeline, and roles. I also confirm what assets I need and what decisions we must make early. Then I create a simple plan in Notion with milestones and review dates. This removes ambiguity and keeps momentum. Good kickoff prevents slowdowns later.
