I start every project with a kickoff focused on removing ambiguity. We clarify goals, success metrics, timeline, roles, and the decisions that need to happen early before design or code begins.

## What we cover in kickoff

**Goals and constraints**
- What success looks like at launch and 90 days out
- Hard deadlines, budget boundaries, and non-negotiables
- Who owns decisions on your side and how fast feedback turns around

**Assets and access**
- Brand files, content, analytics, and existing product context
- Tooling setup: Notion, Slack, Figma, staging environments
- Any integrations, APIs, or third-party accounts I will need

**The project plan**
- Milestones, review dates, and what each phase delivers
- A simple Notion doc you can reference throughout the build
- Risks or dependencies flagged upfront so they do not surprise us later

Good kickoff prevents the slowdowns that show up mid-project when assumptions were never spoken out loud. Teams that skip this step usually lose a week later redoing work that could have been aligned on day one.
