Yes. I design identity systems that can stretch across new products, features, and future launches. I define core rules for typography, color, and layout so everything feels related. Then I add flexible elements like sub-brand patterns or product labels that keep expansion clean. I also plan for real use cases like app icons, landing pages, and product screenshots. Scaling is easier when the system is designed for growth from day one.
