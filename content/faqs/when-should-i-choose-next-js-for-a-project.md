Choose Next.js when you need performance, flexibility, and custom logic beyond a typical builder. I recommend it for products, headless setups, and sites with complex interactions — especially when SEO, speed, and integration with APIs or dynamic content matter at scale.

## Strong fits for Next.js

**Product-grade web apps**
- SaaS front-ends with authenticated routes and dynamic data
- Dashboards and internal tools that need custom UI logic
- MVPs that will grow into full products without a platform migration

**Marketing + content at scale**
- Headless CMS setups with Sanity, Contentful, or similar
- Sites that need strong Core Web Vitals and server-side rendering
- Pages with personalization, localization, or complex routing

## When something else may be better

Next.js is not automatic. Webflow or a simpler stack may be faster for a marketing-only site with no product surface. The key is matching the stack to your operating reality: team skills, maintenance capacity, timeline, and what you are building toward in twelve months.
