Choose Next.js when you need performance, flexibility, and custom logic beyond a typical builder. I recommend it for products, headless setups, and sites with complex interactions. It also works well when you need integration with APIs or dynamic content at scale. If your team can maintain code, it is a strong long-term foundation. The key is matching the stack to your operating reality.
