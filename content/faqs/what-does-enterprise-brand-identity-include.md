Branding is not a logo on a slide. It is a system your team, product, and sales motion can actually use.

## What you receive

**Brand foundation**
- Positioning inputs: what you do, who it is for, and why you win
- Voice direction: how you sound in product, marketing, and sales
- Visual direction: reference set and taste alignment before design starts

**Identity system**
- Logo suite (primary, secondary, and icon mark)
- Typography scale with usage rules
- Color system with primary, secondary, and neutral palettes
- Layout rhythm and hierarchy rules for web, product, and decks

**Assets + guidelines**
- Brand guidelines written for real teams, not shelf-ware
- Ready-to-use assets for web, product UI, social, and sales
- Exported files in the formats your team needs (SVG, PNG, source files)

## Why this matters when multiple teams ship work

When marketing, product, and sales all move in parallel, inconsistency shows up fast: mismatched decks, off-brand product UI, and campaigns that feel like five different companies. A proper identity system reduces rework and makes every touchpoint feel intentional.
