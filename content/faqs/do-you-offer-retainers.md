Yes — for a small number of clients who need ongoing design and development support without re-scoping every request. Retainers work best when you have consistent work, a clear backlog, and want a long-term creative partner who already knows your product and brand.

## What retainers typically cover

**Design support**
- New screens, flow updates, and component additions
- Design system maintenance as the product grows
- Quick-turn explorations for new features or campaigns

**Development support**
- Bug fixes, performance improvements, and small feature builds
- CMS updates, landing pages, and integration tweaks
- Launch support for ongoing releases

**Brand and marketing assets**
- Deck templates, social assets, and campaign landing pages
- Guideline updates as the brand evolves
- Consistency checks across product and marketing

## How retainers are structured

I reserve a set number of hours or sprint days per month, with a shared backlog and async communication so you are not scheduling meetings for every small task. Scope stays flexible within the agreed capacity.

Retainer spots are limited. [Book a call](/book-a-call) to discuss whether ongoing support is the right fit for your team.
