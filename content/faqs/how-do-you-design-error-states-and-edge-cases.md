I design error states by first mapping the most common failures, like invalid inputs, empty data, and permission issues. Then I write clear, human language that explains what happened and what to do next. I also design layouts so errors do not break the page structure. Edge cases are where products feel professional or sloppy. Handling them well reduces support and improves trust.
