Yes. A brand audit is a fast way to identify what is working and what is costing you trust before you invest in a full rebrand. You get clarity on where to spend first.

## What the audit covers

**Consistency across touchpoints**
- Website, product UI, social, sales decks, and email templates
- Logo usage, color drift, typography inconsistencies, and off-brand imagery
- Messaging alignment between what you say and what the visuals communicate

**Deliverable you can act on**
- A prioritized list of fixes ranked by impact and effort
- Quick wins you can ship this month versus deeper changes that need a full project
- Recommended direction if a rebrand or refresh is the right next step

## Why audit before rebuild

Teams often jump straight into a full rebrand when targeted fixes would move the needle faster. An audit helps you invest in the right improvements first instead of starting over unnecessarily. You leave with a clear roadmap, not just a list of problems.
