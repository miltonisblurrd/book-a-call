Brand strategy is the thinking: who you serve, what you promise, why you win, and how you sound. Brand identity is the visual system that expresses that strategy through design. I start with strategy so the identity has a purpose and direction. Then I translate the decisions into typography, color, layout, and marks that feel coherent. When both layers align, your brand becomes easier to trust and easier to market.
