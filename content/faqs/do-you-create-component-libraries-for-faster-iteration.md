Yes. I create a component library in Figma with consistent naming, variants, and responsive rules. This makes new screens faster to design and easier to build. I also align the library with your brand typography and spacing so everything feels cohesive. When teams iterate, consistency usually breaks first. A library protects the product as it grows.
