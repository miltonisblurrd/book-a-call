Yes. I map old URLs to new URLs and implement redirects so a redesign does not cost you months of SEO momentum. SEO protection is a process, not a checkbox you tick on launch day.

## What SEO protection includes

**Pre-launch mapping**
- A full audit of existing URLs, rankings, and inbound links
- 301 redirect rules for renamed, merged, or removed pages
- Preservation of title tags, meta descriptions, and canonical structure where it matters

**Structure and performance**
- Internal linking patterns that keep authority flowing to key pages
- Clean sitemap and robots configuration for the new architecture
- Core Web Vitals checks so speed improvements support rankings, not hurt them

**Post-launch monitoring**
- Broken link checks and Search Console review for crawl errors
- Indexing verification on high-traffic pages
- Quick fixes if unexpected 404s or redirect chains appear

You get a better site without starting SEO from zero. Most traffic dips I see on redesigns come from skipped redirect mapping, not from the new design itself.
