Yes. I map old URLs to new URLs and implement redirects to avoid traffic loss. I also preserve key page structure, metadata, and internal linking where it matters. After launch, I monitor for broken links and indexing issues. SEO protection is a process, not a checkbox. This keeps momentum while the new design ships.
