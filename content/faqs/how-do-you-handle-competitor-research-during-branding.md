I start by mapping your closest competitors and the brands your customers compare you to. I look for repeated visual patterns, messaging clichés, and positioning gaps that you can own. Then I document what to avoid so you do not blend in by accident. I also identify one to two category rules you should follow for trust. The outcome is a direction that feels familiar enough to convert and different enough to remember.
