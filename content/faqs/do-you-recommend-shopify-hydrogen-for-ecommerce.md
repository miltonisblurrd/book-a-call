Yes, when you need full control over storefront UX and performance. Hydrogen is helpful for brands that want custom design, faster experiences, and flexible integrations. It is not necessary for every store, so I evaluate goals and maintenance needs first. If your team wants speed and differentiation, it can be a great fit. The right choice depends on scale and ambition.
