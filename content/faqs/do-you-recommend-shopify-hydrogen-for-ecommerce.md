Yes — when you need full control over storefront UX and performance. Hydrogen is Shopify's React-based headless framework, and it is helpful for brands that want custom design, faster experiences, and flexible integrations without leaving the Shopify ecosystem. It is not necessary for every store, so I evaluate your goals, team capacity, and maintenance needs before recommending it.

## When Hydrogen makes sense

**Custom storefront experience**
- You want a design-forward shop that standard themes cannot deliver
- Performance and Core Web Vitals are a competitive advantage
- You need deep integration with custom front-end logic or APIs

**When a standard Shopify build is enough**
- Smaller catalogs with straightforward checkout flows
- Teams without front-end developers to maintain a headless stack
- Speed-to-launch matters more than full UX customization

## How I evaluate the fit

The right choice depends on scale, ambition, and who will maintain the store after launch. Hydrogen rewards teams that want differentiation and can invest in a modern front-end. A well-built standard Shopify theme may be the smarter move when the product and operations are still finding their footing.
