Most builds take 3 to 8 weeks depending on scope. I move faster when content is ready and decisions are made quickly — especially in sprint formats where timeline and deliverables are locked upfront.

## What affects timeline

**Scope**
- A marketing site with five pages is faster than a web app with auth and dashboards
- Integrations — payments, CMS, analytics, APIs — add time when they are complex
- Custom design vs. adapting an existing system

**Your side**
- Content and assets ready before build starts
- Feedback returned within agreed review windows
- Stakeholder alignment before development begins — not mid-build pivots

**Typical ranges**
- Landing page or focused sprint: 1 to 2 weeks
- Marketing site: 3 to 5 weeks
- Web app or product front end: 5 to 8+ weeks

## Why this matters

Unrealistic timelines lead to cut corners or blown deadlines. Honest scoping upfront keeps quality high and gets you to launch without the chaos of a rushed build.
