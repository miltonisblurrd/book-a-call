Yes. I use skeletons, progressive disclosure, and smart loading states so users feel momentum. I design empty states that explain what is happening and what to do next. I also keep key actions available even when secondary data is loading. Perceived performance is a design problem as much as a backend problem. These details make products feel responsive.
