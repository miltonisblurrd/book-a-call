Yes. I plug into your team for design leadership, front-end execution, or both — whatever fills the gap without creating overlap. We align early on responsibilities so nothing slips between roles.

## How collaboration works

**Clear role definition**
- I take ownership of scoped design or build work while your team handles what they own best
- Shared tools — Figma, Notion, Slack — so everyone works from the same source of truth
- Handoff points defined upfront: what I deliver, when, and in what format

**Working alongside your people**
- Design reviews with your product or engineering leads
- Front-end builds that integrate with your backend or API team
- Documentation and async updates so distributed teams stay aligned across time zones
- Flexible engagement: embedded for a sprint or advisory on a retainer basis

## Why this matters

The worst collaborations happen when roles are fuzzy. When everyone knows who owns what, work moves faster and your internal team keeps building momentum instead of managing confusion.
