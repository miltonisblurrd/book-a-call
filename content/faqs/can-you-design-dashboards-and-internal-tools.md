Yes. Dashboards, admin panels, and internal tools are a core part of my product design work. The goal is not just a clean layout — it is reducing cognitive load so teams can scan data, take action, and move on without fighting the interface.

## What I focus on

**Information hierarchy**
- Group related data so the most important metrics surface first
- Use typography, spacing, and color to guide the eye
- Avoid dashboard sprawl — every widget should earn its place

**Navigation and workflows**
- Clear paths between list views, detail views, and actions
- Role-based UI states so the right people see the right data
- Bulk actions, filters, and search that feel fast

**Data readability**
- Charts and tables designed for glanceability, not decoration
- Empty states, loading states, and error handling built in
- Responsive layouts for teams working across screen sizes

## Common projects

SaaS admin panels, analytics dashboards, CRM interfaces, ops tools, and customer support views. I design for the people who use these tools daily — not just the demo screenshot.
