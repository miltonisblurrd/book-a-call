You do not need a six-week research program to catch obvious friction. I validate design decisions with a lightweight process that checks the right things at the right time — so you ship with confidence, not paralysis.

## How I validate without over-testing

**Goal-first review**
- Does this screen help the user complete their task in fewer steps?
- Are labels, CTAs, and error messages unambiguous?
- Does the flow match how users actually think about the problem?

**Prototype testing with real users**
- 5 to 8 sessions on a clickable prototype is enough to find major issues
- Focus on task completion, not aesthetic preferences
- Test the highest-risk flows first: onboarding, checkout, core actions

**Stakeholder alignment checks**
- Walk through designs against business goals before build starts
- Compare against analytics, support tickets, or sales feedback when available
- Resolve disagreements with evidence, not opinions

## What I skip

Expensive lab studies, surveys about color preferences, and testing every screen before anything ships. The goal is fast signal on the decisions that matter most.
