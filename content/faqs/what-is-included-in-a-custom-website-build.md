I handle design, development, responsive behavior, basic SEO setup, and launch support. You get a site that looks premium, loads fast, and is easy for your team to update — not a template with your logo pasted on top.

## What you receive

**Design + build**
- Custom design aligned with your brand and goals
- Responsive front-end development across real devices and breakpoints
- Clean, maintainable code your team can extend

**Content + CMS**
- CMS setup for pages your team needs to edit — blog, resources, team, etc.
- Reusable sections so marketing can launch new pages without a developer
- Content migration or structure guidance if you are moving from an old site

**Launch readiness**
- Basic SEO: metadata, sitemap, redirects, and structured data where relevant
- Performance-conscious build — optimized assets and efficient code
- QA on core flows, forms, and mobile behavior before go-live
- Post-launch support for critical fixes in the launch window

## Why this matters

Template sites look like templates. A custom build gives you a site that reflects your brand, performs well, and scales with your business — without starting over in a year.
