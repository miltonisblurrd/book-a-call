Yes, on the front-end side. I design and build signup, login, password reset, and account settings screens with clear states and validation. These flows are the first impression for many users — and generic auth UI is one of the fastest ways to make a product feel unfinished, even when the core app is solid.

## What I cover

**Authentication flows**
- Signup, login, password reset, and email verification screens
- Social login and SSO layouts when your provider supports them
- Error handling for expired links, invalid credentials, and rate limits

**Account and settings UI**
- Profile editing, password changes, and notification preferences
- Billing and subscription management surfaces when applicable
- Role or team management UI for multi-user products

## How I approach auth UX

I integrate with your auth provider or backend endpoints — Clerk, Auth0, Supabase, Firebase, or custom APIs. Security-friendly UX matters: clear messaging, safe defaults, and states that do not leak information. The goal is flows that feel polished and trustworthy, not like a template you forgot to customize.
