You need enough to stay consistent — not a 100-page PDF nobody opens. A strong brand identity is a usable system: logo suite, type and color, imagery direction, and clear rules your team can apply without asking you every time.

## Essential deliverables

**Logo suite**
- Primary logo, secondary lockup, and icon mark
- Light, dark, and single-color versions
- Minimum size and clear space rules

**Visual system**
- Typography scale with heading and body pairings
- Color palette: primary, secondary, neutrals, and usage rules
- Layout rhythm and spacing guidelines for web and print

**Guidelines + assets**
- Brand guidelines written for real teams, not shelf-ware
- Ready-to-use assets: social templates, deck layouts, favicon
- Exported files in the formats you need: SVG, PNG, and source files

## What you can skip

Elaborate brand books with filler pages, dozens of logo variations nobody uses, or abstract strategy decks disconnected from the visual system. Focus on what your marketing, product, and sales teams will actually open.
