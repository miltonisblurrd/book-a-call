I keep the work high-touch without filling your calendar. Most progress happens async through Loom and Notion, and I schedule calls only when a decision actually needs live conversation.

## How async keeps things moving

**Updates you can review on your time**
- Weekly written summaries with clear next steps and open asks
- Loom walkthroughs for design reviews, staging previews, and complex flows
- Notion docs that stay updated so you always have a single source of truth

**Meetings reserved for high-stakes moments**
- Direction lock at the start of a major phase
- Major design or build reviews where real-time feedback saves rounds
- Launch readiness checks before go-live

## The result

You stay closely involved in the work without losing your week to status calls. Decisions get documented, feedback loops stay short, and the process still feels personal. That balance works well for founders and lean teams who need visibility without a full-time meeting load.
