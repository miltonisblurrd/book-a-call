I keep meetings minimal and use async updates for most progress. I schedule calls only for key decisions like direction lock, major reviews, and launch readiness. Between calls, I use Loom and Notion so you can review on your time. This keeps the work high-touch without being meeting-heavy. You stay informed without losing your week.
