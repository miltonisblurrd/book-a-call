Product design is an investment in clarity: fewer rebuilds, faster stakeholder alignment, and interfaces your team can actually ship.

## How pricing works

Design is scoped in **sprints** based on:
- Number of flows and screens required for launch
- New product UI vs redesign of existing surfaces
- Prototype depth and exploration rounds
- Whether a design system or component library is part of scope

## What drives the number

The scope conversation covers:
- MVP launch vs full product redesign
- Web app, dashboard, or marketing-only scope
- How many teams will use the files (product, marketing, eng)
- Timeline and review structure

## Getting a right-sized quote

You do not need a perfect brief to start. A short discovery call is enough to recommend the right sprint plan: a focused flow redesign, or a full launch-ready UI package.
