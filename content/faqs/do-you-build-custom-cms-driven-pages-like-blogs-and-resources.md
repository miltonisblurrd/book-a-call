Yes. I set up structured content types so blogs, FAQs, and resource pages are easy to add and maintain. A good CMS setup is an investment in speed that pays off every time your team publishes.

## What CMS-driven pages include

**Structured content types**
- Blog posts, case studies, FAQs, and resource libraries with defined fields
- Templates that keep typography, spacing, and layout consistent across every entry
- Category, tag, and filtering systems that scale as content grows

**Editor-friendly implementation**
- Clean CMS fields so editors are not overwhelmed by options
- Preview workflows so content can be reviewed before publish
- SEO fields for titles, descriptions, and social sharing built into the editor

**Design that holds up at volume**
- Listing pages with pagination or load-more patterns
- Detail pages with readable type scale and comfortable line length
- Related content and CTA blocks to keep readers moving through the site

Content marketing only works if publishing stays sustainable. I typically work with headless CMS options like Sanity or Contentful, depending on your team size and workflow preferences.
