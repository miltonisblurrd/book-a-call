Yes. I diagnose what is slowing your site down and prioritize fixes that improve LCP, CLS, and INP without breaking the design you already have. Better vitals usually improve SEO and conversion at the same time.

## What I look for first

**Common performance bottlenecks**
- Unoptimized images and missing lazy loading
- Heavy third-party scripts bloating initial load
- Layout shifts from fonts, ads, or dynamically injected content
- Render-blocking CSS and JavaScript on above-the-fold content

**Fixes prioritized by impact**
- Image compression, modern formats, and responsive sizing
- Font loading strategy with proper fallbacks to reduce CLS
- Script deferral and removal of unnecessary third-party tags
- Component-level optimizations for interaction responsiveness (INP)

## After changes ship

I retest with Lighthouse and real-world metrics to confirm improvement, not just theoretical gains. Speed work should be measurable, and I share before-and-after numbers so you can see the impact clearly. Most fixes are front-end and do not require a full rebuild.
