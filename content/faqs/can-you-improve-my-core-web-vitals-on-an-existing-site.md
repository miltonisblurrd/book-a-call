Yes. I diagnose what is slowing the site down, like heavy scripts, unoptimized images, or layout shifts. Then I prioritize fixes that improve LCP, CLS, and INP without breaking the design. I also reduce unnecessary third-party bloat where possible. After changes, I retest to confirm improvement. Better vitals usually improves SEO and conversion at the same time.
