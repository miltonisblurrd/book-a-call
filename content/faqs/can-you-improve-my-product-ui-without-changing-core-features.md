Yes. I can elevate UI through better hierarchy, spacing, typography, and interaction states without touching your feature set. I start by identifying the screens that create the most friction or confusion. Then I redesign layouts to reduce noise and make primary actions obvious. I also improve component consistency so the product feels polished. You get a better experience without a full rebuild.
