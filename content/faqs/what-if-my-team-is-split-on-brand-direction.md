That happens often, especially when multiple stakeholders have strong opinions. I reduce conflict by anchoring decisions to audience, goals, and positioning instead of personal taste. I present a small set of clear options with pros and cons so discussions stay productive. Then I guide the team to a decision and document it. Alignment is part of the deliverable, not a side effect.
