Yes. I deliver guidelines that explain how to use the logo, typography, colors, and layout rules. The goal is consistency across web, product, and marketing — so anyone on your team can apply the brand correctly without asking me every time.

## What guidelines include

**Visual rules**
- Logo usage: clear space, minimum sizes, do's and don'ts
- Typography: typefaces, sizes, weights, and hierarchy
- Color palette with hex values and usage context
- Imagery and icon style direction

**Application guidance**
- Layout principles for web, decks, and social
- Voice and tone notes where relevant
- Examples of correct and incorrect usage
- File formats and asset exports your team can reference
- Guidance for extending the system as new channels or products appear

## Why this matters

A brand without guidelines drifts the moment more than one person touches it. Clear rules protect the investment you made in the identity and keep everything looking intentional as you grow and add new channels.
