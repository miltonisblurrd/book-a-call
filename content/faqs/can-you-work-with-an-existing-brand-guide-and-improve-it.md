Yes. I review your current guidelines to find what is unclear, inconsistent, or hard to apply in real work. Many brand guides were built for a launch moment — not for the day-to-day reality of designers, marketers, and developers trying to ship. I simplify the rules, refine the visuals, and fill the gaps that cause drift.

## What I typically improve

**Clarity and usability**
- Rules rewritten so non-designers can follow them
- Examples tied to actual surfaces: web, product, email, social
- Do-and-don't guidance instead of vague aesthetic language

**Visual refinement**
- Logo usage, color, and typography tightened where the system has loosened
- Missing components added: icons, illustration, photography direction
- Updated asset kit with the right files in the right formats

**Flexibility without chaos**
- Rigid guides made more practical for growing teams
- Guardrails that prevent drift without blocking good work
- Product and marketing applications covered — not just stationery mockups

The goal is a guide people actually follow, not a PDF that lives in a shared drive and gets ignored.
