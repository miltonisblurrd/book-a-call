Yes. I align the landing page with your product UI so the experience feels continuous from first click to first session inside the app. Consistency here increases trust and reduces bounce.

## How landing and product stay connected

**Shared visual language**
- Matching typography, color, spacing rhythm, and component patterns
- Product screenshots or UI mockups styled to feel native, not pasted in
- Motion and interaction cues that preview how the app actually behaves

**Conversion structure built around the journey**
- Hero and proof sections tied to the core problem you solve
- Feature blocks that mirror the in-app experience users will see
- CTAs placed at decision points, not just at the bottom of the page

**Performance and SEO from day one**
- Fast load times and clean metadata so the page ranks and converts
- Responsive layouts that look sharp on every device
- Analytics hooks to measure traffic quality and signup rates

If your landing page and app feel like two different products, that gap costs you conversions. I treat the landing as the first screen of the product experience, not a separate marketing artifact.
