          <p class="text-paragraph u-extra-bold margin-19">Our Involvement:</p>
          <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray">BLURRD first partnered with ShipNetwork in 2022 when Rakuten Super Logistics became ShipNetwork—a full rebrand and website launch under a tight deadline. When the company returned for a second engagement, the scope was different. This was not a relaunch. It was a strategic reset: a complete brand identity focused on client experience, a ground-up rebuild of a 700+ page Webflow Enterprise site, and a marketing model that balances lead generation with brand awareness and long-cycle nurture.</p>
          <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">The work spanned identity, messaging, tone, color, social creative, website redesign, custom HubSpot map widgets, content funnel architecture, and three persona-specific ad landing pages—each designed to meet a distinct buyer where they are.</p>

            <h2 class="h2">The Brand Shift</h2>
          </div>
          <div class="wrapper-blue u-mb-2 u-scroll-none">
            <div class="wrapper-blue-header u-p-20-around">
              <div class="wrapper-header u-mb-0">
                <p class="text-paragraph u-extra-bold u-text-white u-mb-0 u-mr-2">From conversion-first to client-first</p>
              </div>
            </div>
            <div class="u-p-20-around">
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray">ShipNetwork operates at serious scale—nationwide fulfillment centers, proprietary carrier optimization (KNCT), real-time WMS dashboards, and dedicated POD support teams for every client. But the old brand did not reflect that human side. The site was strict, funnel-heavy, and optimized for one outcome: form submission.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">The new identity reframes ShipNetwork as a partner, not a vendor. The headline—<em>The 3PL That Actually Cares About Your Experience</em>—anchors a brand built on personalized support, authentic communication, and the feeling that ShipNetwork runs fulfillment as an extension of your team. That shift shows up in copy, layout rhythm, photography direction, and the way CTAs are balanced with educational content.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">Leads still matter. ShipNetwork still sells high-value fulfillment contracts. But the brand now also serves buyers who need time—people researching guides, comparing 3PLs, downloading ebooks, or subscribing to a newsletter before they are ready to talk. The experience had to support both the fast converter and the long nurture.</p>

Social proof, team photography, and client testimonials became structural elements, not afterthoughts.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">Updated colors and design patterns carry the new tone across the site, social channels, and sales collateral—giving ShipNetwork's internal marketing team a consistent system to extend without drifting back into the old conversion-only aesthetic.</p>

          </div>
          <div class="wrapper-blue u-mb-2 u-scroll-none">

              </div>
            </div>
            <div class="u-p-20-around">
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray">This was not a reskin. ShipNetwork's site is 700+ pages deep—service pages, industry verticals, location pages, resource hubs, guides, technology pages, and enterprise content built over years of growth. BLURRD rebuilt the platform from the ground up on Webflow Enterprise, using the full feature set to manage a site at this scale.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">The previous build had accumulated serious technical debt: 1,400+ class names, redundant styles, and bloated CSS that made updates slow and inconsistent. The rebuild consolidated that down to just under 1,000 class names—a nearly 30% reduction—while preserving page structure, URL patterns, and the SEO equity ShipNetwork depends on.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">New reusable templates and components give the marketing team faster publishing workflows. Service cards, testimonial modules, stat blocks, resource grids, and fulfillment network sections are built once and deployed across hundreds of pages—so brand updates propagate without manual page-by-page fixes.</p>

ShipNetwork now invests in brand awareness and education alongside direct conversion—guides, ebooks, newsletters, and resource hubs that nurture buyers who are still evaluating.</p>

CTAs are tiered: high-intent paths to request a quote sit alongside lower-commitment options—download a guide, explore the fulfillment network, subscribe to updates. Layouts make room for content depth instead of compressing every page into a single funnel step.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">That shift supports ShipNetwork's actual sales motion. Many of their best clients come from referrals and long relationships—buyers who needed to trust the partner before signing. The site now reflects that reality.</p>

Generic landing pages were not going to move the needle. Each persona has different pain points, decision criteria, and language—and ad traffic deserved pages built specifically for them.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">BLURRD designed and built three separate ad landing pages—each with its own layout, messaging emphasis, proof points, and visual tone tuned to that persona. One page might lead with dedicated client support and onboarding ease. Another emphasizes scale, peak-volume performance, and enterprise reliability. A third speaks to shipping cost optimization and delivery speed for fast-growing DTC brands.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">The result is higher relevance from first click: visitors see themselves in the headline, the imagery, and the outcomes—not a one-size-fits-all fulfillment pitch.</p>

            <div class="u-p-20-around">
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray">ShipNetwork's fulfillment network is a core selling point—centers across the U.S. delivering 1–2 day ground reach to 98% of the continental U.S. Showing that network on the site needed to go beyond static maps. BLURRD built custom map widgets from scratch—100% custom, not off-the-shelf embeds—and connected them directly to ShipNetwork's HubSpot CRM.</p>
              <p class="text-paragraph u-text-gray u-mt-2">That integration means location data, lead routing, and CRM workflows stay in sync. When a prospect explores fulfillment centers on the map, the experience can reflect real operational data and feed intelligence back into HubSpot for sales and marketing follow-up. It is the kind of bespoke functionality enterprise Webflow makes possible when paired with a clear integration strategy.</p>
